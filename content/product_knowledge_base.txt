ABOUT Test AI ACADEMY
Test AI Academy is an ed-tech platform offering cohort-based learning programs in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science for working professionals, students, and career-switchers. Our flagship offering combines live instruction, hands-on projects, and mentorship to help learners build real, job-ready skills.

CORE PRODUCT OFFERINGS

1. Cohort-Based Courses
Live, instructor-led programs delivered over 3 to 12 weeks, capped at 40-50 learners per cohort to preserve interactivity. Sessions are held twice a week (typically evenings, IST) and are recorded for later viewing. Each cohort includes:
- Live lectures with Q&A
- Hands-on assignments graded within 5 business days
- A capstone project reviewed by instructors
- Access to a private cohort Slack/Discord channel for peer discussion

Current flagship courses include:
- "Applied Machine Learning for Professionals" (6 weeks)
- "Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Systems" (3 weeks)
- "Data Science Foundations" (8 weeks)
- "MLOps and Production AI Systems" (4 weeks)

2. Self-Paced Courses
Pre-recorded video modules with quizzes and auto-graded coding exercises, available for lifetime access after purchase. These are priced lower than cohort programs and serve as an entry point for learners not ready to commit to a live schedule.

3. Mentorship Program
1:1 mentorship sessions (30 or 60 minutes) with industry practitioners, bookable through the platform's mentorship marketplace. Mentors are vetted by the Academic Team and rated by learners after each session.

4. Career Services
Available as an add-on to select cohort programs, including resume reviews, mock interviews, and access to a hiring partner network. This is not a placement guarantee and is positioned as "career support," not "job placement," in all learner-facing communication.

PRICING TIERS (INDICATIVE)
- Self-paced courses: INR 2,999 – INR 7,999
- Cohort-based courses: INR 15,000 – INR 45,000 depending on duration and career services add-ons
- Mentorship sessions: INR 999 – INR 2,999 per session depending on mentor seniority
- Corporate/team licenses: Custom pricing, negotiated by the Enterprise Sales team

REFUND POLICY
Learners may request a full refund within 7 days of the cohort start date if fewer than 2 live sessions have been attended. After this window, refunds are prorated based on sessions remaining, minus a 10% processing fee. Self-paced course purchases are refundable within 14 days if less than 20% of the content has been consumed.

LEARNING PLATFORM FEATURES
- Adaptive quizzes that adjust difficulty based on learner performance.
- Progress dashboard showing module completion, assignment scores, and attendance.
- Certificate of completion issued upon meeting minimum attendance (70%) and assignment completion (80%) thresholds.
- Discussion forums per cohort, moderated by teaching assistants.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT
Learner support is available via in-app chat (9 AM - 9 PM IST, all days) and email at support@Testaiacademy.com, with a target first-response time of 4 hours during support hours. Technical issues (video playback, login) are escalated to the Platform Engineering team with a 24-hour resolution SLA.

KEY METRICS THE PRODUCT TEAM TRACKS
- Cohort completion rate (target: above 75%)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS) per cohort, collected via post-course survey
- Assignment turnaround time for graders
- Mentorship session repeat-booking rate

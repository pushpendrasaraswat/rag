Test AI ACADEMY — HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY
Document Version: 2.3
Last Updated: January 2026
Owner: People Operations Team

1. WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
Standard working hours at Test AI Academy are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Employees are expected to log at least 8 productive hours per day via the internal time-tracking tool, HoursHub. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, during which all employees are expected to be reachable for meetings.

Remote work is permitted up to 3 days per week for all full-time employees, subject to manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require Director-level sign-off and are reviewed every 6 months.

2. LEAVE POLICY
Employees accrue 1.5 days of paid leave per month, totaling 18 days annually. Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days; anything beyond this cap is forfeited at year-end.

- Sick Leave: 10 days annually, no carry-forward.
- Casual Leave: 8 days annually, can be availed with 24 hours' notice.
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks as per statutory guidelines.
- Paternity Leave: 15 days, to be availed within 6 months of childbirth.
- Bereavement Leave: 5 days for immediate family.

All leave requests must be submitted through the LeaveDesk portal at least 3 working days in advance, except for sick leave and emergencies.

3. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees are expected to maintain professionalism in all interactions, whether in-person, virtual, or written. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated and should be reported immediately to People Operations or via the anonymous EthicsLine.

Conflicts of interest, including outside employment or advisory roles, must be disclosed to your reporting manager and People Operations within 15 days of onboarding or occurrence.

4. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year — a mid-year check-in in June and an annual review in December. Reviews are based on a 5-point rating scale (Needs Improvement, Developing, Meets Expectations, Exceeds Expectations, Outstanding) and factor in peer feedback, manager assessment, and self-evaluation.

Promotions are considered during the December review cycle, and salary revisions are typically effective from April 1st of the following financial year.

5. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
All employees receive:
- Group health insurance covering self, spouse, and up to 2 dependent children.
- Annual health check-up reimbursement up to INR 8,000.
- Meal allowance of INR 2,500 per month via a prepaid card.
- Internet and home-office reimbursement of INR 1,500 per month for remote-eligible roles.
- Learning and Development stipend of INR 25,000 per year for courses, certifications, or conference attendance.

6. EXIT POLICY
The standard notice period is 60 days for all confirmed employees and 15 days during the probation period (first 90 days of employment). Exit interviews are mandatory and are scheduled by People Operations within the final two weeks of the notice period. Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.

7. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
Any employee with a grievance related to compensation, working conditions, or interpersonal conflict may escalate through their reporting manager, and if unresolved, to People Operations. A formal Internal Committee handles complaints related to workplace harassment as per statutory requirements, with a resolution timeline of 90 days.

For questions regarding this policy, contact hr-policy@testaiacademy.com.
